FAQ

Questions, answered

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for hard surfaces like concrete, brick and stone. Soft washing uses low pressure with gentle detergents for siding, stucco, roofs and painted surfaces — it removes mold and algae at the root without risking damage.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — fully insured, and we're happy to provide a certificate of insurance before we start any job.

Do I need to be home during the service?

For most exterior work, no. We just need access to water and the areas being cleaned. For interior window cleaning, we'll schedule a time when you're available.

Are your detergents safe for plants and pets?

We use biodegradable, exterior-rated detergents and rinse landscaping before and after washing to protect your plants.

How do quotes work?

They're free and fast. Send the form or text us photos — most quotes go out the same day, and the price we quote is the price you pay.
